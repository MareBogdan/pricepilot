Competitive pricing intelligence

Track competitors. Match their listings. Price with a hard margin floor.

PricePilot collects competitor prices from Romanian pet shops every day, uses a fine-tuned model to work out which competitor listing is the same product as ours, retrieves our pricing policy, and has an LLM propose a price that plain code then checks against the margin floor.
